# The Network Council

The council stewards the constitution: it ratifies the movement's operating rules, reviews pilot evidence, protects the three community rules, and ensures the movement is designed to be outgrown, including by its founder.

## How it works

- Quarterly rhythm, plus the founding ratification session
- Decisions in the open: ratifications and amendments happen on GitHub issues and pull requests
- Term-limited like every leadership role in the movement; the council's own succession follows the same rules it ratifies for chapters
